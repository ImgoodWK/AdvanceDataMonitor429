## Author's project story

The project was conceived in spring 2025 and formed its complete
**AdvanceDataMonitor** direction in May. Advice
from a few enthusiasts helped shape the monitor, storage, and automation
directions. Later in 2025, other responsibilities made it practical to keep
the work private for a period. A surgery in early 2026 further delayed hands-on
development. In April 2026 the project was reorganized into a new public form;
AI-assisted development then became part of the workflow, and the project was
gradually renamed **TeXTech**.

This personal account explains the pauses and rename from the maintainer's
perspective. The Git table above remains the citation authority for exact
commit dates.
