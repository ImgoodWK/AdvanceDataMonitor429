# TeXTech community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We pledge to make participation in TeXTech a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive, specific feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for players, contributors, and the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized
attention, trolling, insults, threats, deliberate exposure of private
information, knowingly malicious code, credential disclosure, and sustained
disruption of project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository Issues, pull requests, Discussions, Wiki,
release comments, and other spaces where someone officially represents the
project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, close
threads, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior
is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report a conduct concern privately by contacting
[@ImgoodWK](https://github.com/ImgoodWK) and requesting a private channel.
Do not repost private information in a public Issue. Reports will be reviewed
with attention to the safety and privacy of the reporter and affected people.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
